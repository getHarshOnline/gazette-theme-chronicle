# Working on Chronicle

Read [WORLD.md](WORLD.md) for the accepted portrait and unfinished chronological studies, then [Compositions](docs/compositions.md) for the selectors and source bindings. Apply `gazette-theme-base`’s `AGENTS.md` for shared behavior, ownership, neutral fixtures and verification. Pale’s [design guidance](https://github.com/getHarshOnline/pale/blob/main/packages/design/INDEX.md) gives the method, and its [browser task](https://github.com/getHarshOnline/pale/blob/main/packages/design/browser-task.md) page gives the browser review route.

Chronicle owns presentation, `design-tokens` owns values, and the workspace supplies identity, content and registered portrait sources. Follow the [build and check route](README.md#build-and-check); change values at the producer, not in generated captures.

Preserve likeness and registration, the complete head and the single choice rail. Source geometry owns the portrait; Base owns response and reveal. Keep decorative response separate from keyboard focus. About’s length does not call for an article outline; chronology belongs only where it explains the account. Illustrated pursuits do not establish ownership.

Check recognition without accent and held light in both modes. Fixtures use fictional content; presentation preserves facts, units, sources, status and destinations. Report engine gaps to Base or Gazette’s owner while retaining the approved treatment. Implementation and checks do not constitute approval. Work in this repository; the workspace chooses what renders.
